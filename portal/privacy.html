Privacy Policy & Data Security Standards

1. Strict Zero Prompt Logging & Ephemeral Volatile Processing

ComputeMesh operates under a strict Zero-Log architecture. User prompts and generated model responses are not written to persistent disks, not stored in databases, and not used for model training. Inference activations exist in volatile GPU VRAM during real-time streaming and are discarded upon request completion.

2. End-to-End Encryption in Transit (TLS 1.3 & mTLS)

All communications between client applications and our edge gateways are strictly encrypted via TLS 1.3 with Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS). Internal communications across distributed provider nodes use mutual TLS (mTLS) with cryptographically authenticated Root CA certificates and node identity gating, preventing eavesdropping, tampering, or man-in-the-middle attacks.

3. Data-at-Rest Security (AES-256-GCM / Encrypted Host Volumes)

Identity vaults and credential stores are encrypted using industry-standard AES-256-GCM / PBKDF2. Persistent ledger state and service configurations operate on secure encrypted host volumes. Cryptographic keys and administrative tokens are isolated in protected server environments.

4. Provider Private Key Isolation & Hardware Security

Hardware provider nodes generate their asymmetric Ed25519 keypairs locally upon first boot. The private key NEVER leaves the local machine, is never transmitted to ComputeMesh servers, and is never accessible to consumers or outside network peers.

5. PCI-DSS Certified Payment & Escrow Security

All real customer payment processing is handled through Stripe, Inc., certified under PCI-DSS Level 1. ComputeMesh never sees, stores, or handles unencrypted payment card details or banking credentials. Crypto wallet addresses are stored solely as public provider payout destination keys and are not used to charge customers.

6. GDPR / DSGVO Compliance & Data Subject Rights

Users maintain full rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / DSGVO). You may request full data export, correction, or permanent account deletion at any time by contacting our data protection officer at privacy@inetconnector.com.
