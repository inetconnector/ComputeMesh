Privacy Policy

1. Zero Prompt Logging Guarantee

ComputeMesh does not store, train on, or inspect user prompts or model completions. Inference activations are streamed in-memory across pipeline layers and discarded immediately upon generation.

2. Telemetry & Metering

We collect only anonymous technical telemetry required for scheduler placement and double-entry accounting: token counts, model identifiers, GPU hardware temperatures, and network round-trip latencies.

3. Provider Key Security

Provider nodes generate and hold asymmetric Ed25519 private keys locally on their hardware. Keys are never transmitted over the network or stored by ComputeMesh servers.

4. GDPR & Data Subject Rights

Users can request account data deletion or export of billing ledger history at any time by contacting support.
