Terms of Service & Provider Settlement Rules

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using the ComputeMesh decentralized compute platform, API gateway, or provider node software, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service.

2. API Usage & Compute Credits (Consumers)

Developers and enterprise customers purchase prepaid compute credits in USD, EUR, or crypto (USDC/USDT). Consumption is billed strictly per processed token metered by our verifiable double-entry ledger.

3. Hardware Provider Obligations

Hardware providers supply GPU compute capacity using official ComputeMesh binaries. Providers are responsible for maintaining stable connectivity and truthful node telemetry during active sessions.

4. Provider Earnings, Real-Time Accounting, Fully-Backed Settlement & 2-Tier Payout System ($25.00 Threshold)

For each successfully served inference token, a revenue share (85% of metered token price) is credited in micro-units to the provider's ledger account in real-time. Payout entitlements operate strictly under the fully-backed revenue principle: cash withdrawals are disbursed exclusively from cleared, non-reversible consumer payments actually received and settled on the platform's merchant account (Stripe or on-chain crypto treasury). No pre-financing liability or unbacked withdrawal claims exist. Payouts trigger at a minimum balance of $25.00 USD (or EUR / USDC / USDT equivalent) via either (A) instant crypto transfer (USDC/USDT) to a registered 0x... EVM wallet, or (B) SEPA bank transfer via Stripe Connect Express.

5. Network Bootstrap Phase, Alpha Testing & Promotional Test Credits

During the initial bootstrap and growth phase of ComputeMesh, compute workload is dynamically routed according to organic model demand. Free promotional credits, trial balances, and testnet tokens granted to consumers for testing purposes constitute non-monetary test allocations and explicitly do not generate cash withdrawal liabilities against the platform operator. Earnings derived from paid customer usage never expire and remain permanently ledger-recorded until the $25.00 threshold is reached.

6. Payment Service Providers, Stripe Express & Escrow Management

All fiat transactions and SEPA provider bank payouts are processed through our licensed partner Stripe, Inc. (and Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd.) via Stripe Connect Express with automatic bank payee verification. On-chain crypto payouts (USDT/USDC) are executed directly via decentralized EVM smart contract networks (Arbitrum, Polygon, Base, Ethereum). The platform operator inetconnector maintains the orchestration software, edge gateways, and immutable double-entry ledger that meter token consumption and issue payout orders.

7. Limitation of Liability & Disclaimers

ComputeMesh provides decentralized orchestration software on an 'as-is' and 'as-available' basis without warranties of uninterrupted uptime or constant node utilization.
