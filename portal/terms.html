Terms of Service & Provider Settlement Rules

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using the ComputeMesh decentralized compute platform, API gateway, or provider node software, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service.

2. API Usage & Compute Credits (Consumers)

Developers and enterprise customers purchase prepaid compute credits in USD, EUR, or crypto (USDC/USDT). Consumption is billed strictly per processed token metered by our verifiable double-entry ledger.

3. Hardware Provider Obligations

Hardware providers supply GPU compute capacity using official ComputeMesh binaries. Providers are responsible for maintaining stable connectivity and truthful node telemetry during active sessions.

4. Provider Earnings, Real-Time Accounting & Minimum Payout Threshold ($25.00)

For each successfully served inference token, micro-credits are recorded in real-time to the provider's double-entry ledger account. To prevent disproportionate blockchain gas fees and payment processing charges on micro-transactions, automated payouts (via EVM wallet or SEPA) require a minimum accumulated balance of $25.00 USD (or equivalent EUR / USDC / USDT). Once your balance meets or exceeds this threshold, earnings are dispatched in the next automated settlement batch to your registered wallet address or IBAN.

5. Early Network Bootstrap & Ramp-Up Phase

During the initial bootstrap and growth phase of ComputeMesh, incoming inference workload is dynamically distributed across active nodes according to model demand. All earned credits are permanent, non-expiring, and securely preserved on the ledger until the $25.00 threshold is reached. No fixed daily yield or minimum capacity utilization is guaranteed during early network bootstrapping.

6. Payment Service Providers & Fund Management

All fiat transactions (Credit Card, SEPA, Apple Pay) are securely processed and held in escrow by our licensed payment partner Stripe, Inc. (and Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd.). On-chain crypto transactions (USDT/USDC) are settled directly via decentralized EVM smart contract networks (Arbitrum, Polygon, Base, Ethereum) to the provider's registered wallet. The platform operator inetconnector provides the orchestration software, edge gateways, and immutable double-entry ledger that calculate token usage and issue settlement instructions.

7. Limitation of Liability & Disclaimers

ComputeMesh provides decentralized orchestration software on an 'as-is' and 'as-available' basis without warranties of uninterrupted uptime or constant node utilization.
